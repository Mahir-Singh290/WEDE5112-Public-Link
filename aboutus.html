Founded in 1933 by Philip Krawitz, Cape Union Mart originated as an "Army and Navy Store" in Cape Town. Over decades of innovation, quality service, and expansion, Cape Union Mart evolved into South Africa's favorite outdoor retail store, bringing top local and international brands to outdoor enthusiasts.

To equip our customers with high-performance gear and apparel, empowering everyone to explore, discover, and enjoy the great outdoors safely and comfortably.

To remain South Africa's leading outdoor lifestyle retailer by inspiring adventure, delivering exceptional quality, and pioneering sustainable outdoor practices.
